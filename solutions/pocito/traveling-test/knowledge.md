# Northstar Loom Tel Aviv Delegation

This file is the benchmark's shared background dossier. Every person and company below is fictional. Real venues are used only as trip locations.

## Company

Northstar Loom is a London-based B2B software company founded in 2022. Its product, LoomRoute, turns fragmented fleet, warehouse, and supplier
signals into explainable disruption plans. Operations teams can compare alternatives, override a recommendation, and preserve the decision trail.

The company has 31 employees, is preparing for a Series A, and sells annual software subscriptions with paid implementation. Its current customers
are mid-market European logistics operators. The Tel Aviv trip is intended to expand the product into fleet and port operations.

## Trip goals

1. Agree a 90-day design-partner pilot with Cedar Mobility.
2. Validate a port-logistics integration and technical sandbox with BlueHarbor Systems.
3. Establish a relationship with Dune Ventures before Northstar Loom's Series A.
4. Test the redacted LoomRoute demo with realistic but synthetic shipment data.

The Cedar pilot is successful if dispatchers receive earlier warnings, compare explainable alternatives, retain manual override, and measurably reduce
late-delivery decisions. No production credentials or customer shipment records may be used during the trip.

## Commercial and technical context

- Cedar wants human-readable reasons, manual override, audit history, and pilot success metrics agreed before kickoff.
- BlueHarbor wants rate-limit behavior, idempotent webhook retries, EU/Israel data residency, retention boundaries, and a sandbox before production work.
- Dune is focused on runway, pipeline concentration, defensibility, implementation effort, and the rate at which pilots become annual contracts.
- Northstar's demo contains synthetic shipments. SSO, audit logs, retention, and least-privilege access are in scope; production credentials are not.
