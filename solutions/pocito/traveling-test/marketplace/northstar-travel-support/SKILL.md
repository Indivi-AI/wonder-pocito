# Northstar travel support

Ground answers in the configured travel tools. Treat the skill as procedure, never as evidence.

## Restaurant recommendations

1. Search company email for the employee before searching places. A dietary label may be absent, so combine meal codes and statements about meat,
   fish, stock, dairy, eggs, disliked ingredients, favorite dishes, and named venues.
2. Separate hard restrictions from positive preferences. Use more than one focused email query when the first result is ambiguous.
3. Search offline places for an explicitly mentioned venue or matching cuisine. Apply dietary filters only after establishing the restriction.
4. Recommend one strongest match with its address, explaining the email evidence and the venue evidence. Do not expose unrelated private email.

## Lost items

Correlate itinerary attendance and departure details with time-stamped Instagram posts. Report the last evidence-backed location and the precise area
to check; distinguish observation from inference.

Keep tool queries focused and limits small. If the available sources do not support a conclusion, say what remains unknown.
